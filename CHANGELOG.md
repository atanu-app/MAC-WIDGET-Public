# Changelog

All notable changes to KairoWidget are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-06

The first public release.

### Widgets
- **CPU** — total usage, user/system split, live graph and per-core bars; click for 10 minutes of history and the top apps.
- **Memory** — memory used with a pressure-coloured bar, wired, compressed and swap; click for history and the top apps.
- **Processes** — the top 8 apps by CPU or memory.
- **Network Access** — every port in use, the project or app behind it, and whether only this Mac or your network can reach it; click to open in your browser.
- **Network** — upload and download speed with a live graph.
- **Disk** — free space and read/write speed.
- **Battery** — charge, time remaining, health, cycle count, charger wattage and Energy Mode.
- **Temperature** — chip average, hottest sensor, battery and SSD.
- **GPU & Fans** — GPU usage and fan speeds.
- **Wi-Fi** — network name, signal, speed, band, Wi-Fi generation and IP address.

### Features
- Pin widgets to the desktop or float them on top; positions are remembered.
- Menu-bar readout of CPU, temperature, memory and network speed.
- Alerts for low memory, high temperature, low battery and newly exposed network ports.
- Stop a project's dev server from Network Access, with a confirmation that shows exactly what will be stopped.
- Launch at Login.
- Refresh every 1, 2 or 5 seconds.
